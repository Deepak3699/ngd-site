# 15 Best AI Tools in 2027 for Work, Research and Creativity

Artificial intelligence is changing how people work, study, create content and run businesses. Tasks that once required hours of research, writing, designing or editing can now be assisted by AI tools.

The challenge is choosing the right tool. Some AI products are designed for writing and research, while others specialise in image generation, video creation, voiceovers or business automation.

This guide covers 15 useful AI tools across these categories. For each tool, you will find what it does, who may benefit from it and whether a free option is available.

**A note about 2027:** AI products, features and prices change frequently. This guide focuses on tools and capabilities available around the time of publication. Check each official website for current plan details before subscribing. A free plan may have usage limits and does not necessarily include every feature.

## 1. ChatGPT — Best all-round AI assistant

[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) is a general-purpose AI assistant from OpenAI. It can help with writing, brainstorming, learning, analysis, planning and many everyday tasks.

**What you can use it for:**
- Draft emails, articles, reports and social media content.
- Break down difficult topics into simpler explanations.
- Work with supported files and data.
- Brainstorm business ideas and marketing plans.
- Generate and edit images where supported.
- Assist with coding and problem-solving.

**Practical example:** A small business owner can use ChatGPT to draft product descriptions, plan a month of social media topics and prepare answers to common customer questions.

**Free or paid?** ChatGPT has a free plan. Paid plans offer different limits and additional capabilities. Availability can change by plan and region.

**Best for:** Students, professionals, creators, developers and business owners.

## 2. Claude — Best for writing and long-document analysis

[Claude](https://claude.ai/) is an AI assistant from Anthropic. It is useful for detailed writing, working through complex instructions and analysing documents.

**What you can use it for:**
- Draft and improve long-form content.
- Summarise lengthy documents.
- Analyse supported uploaded files.
- Organise research and ideas.
- Explain complex subjects.
- Assist with coding and problem-solving.

**Practical example:** If you have a lengthy project report, Claude can help you summarise sections, identify key points and improve clarity. You should still verify its interpretation against the original report.

**Free or paid?** Claude offers free and paid plans with different usage limits and features.

**Best for:** Writers, researchers, students and professionals who work with detailed documents.

## 3. Google Gemini — Best for Google's AI ecosystem

[Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) is Google's AI assistant. It can work with different kinds of input, including text and images, depending on the available model and features.

**What you can use it for:**
- Ask questions and learn new concepts.
- Draft and improve content.
- Understand supported images and documents.
- Brainstorm ideas.
- Assist with research and creative tasks.
- Use supported integrations with Google services.

**Practical example:** A student can share a picture of a difficult concept and ask Gemini to explain it in simple language with an example.

**Free or paid?** Gemini provides free access, while advanced capabilities and higher limits may require a paid Google AI plan. Features vary by region and subscription.

**Best for:** Students, professionals and people who use Google's ecosystem.

## 4. Microsoft Copilot — Best for everyday productivity

[Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com/) is Microsoft's AI assistant. It can help users draft content, explore ideas, summarise information and complete supported productivity tasks.

**What you can use it for:**
- Draft and rewrite text.
- Summarise information.
- Brainstorm and research topics.
- Assist with productivity tasks.
- Use AI features in supported Microsoft products.

**Practical example:** A professional can use Copilot to prepare a first draft of a report or organise ideas for a presentation.

**Free or paid?** Copilot has free consumer access. Microsoft 365 Copilot features for work and business have separate eligibility and licensing requirements.

**Best for:** Office users, professionals, students and organisations using Microsoft products.

## 5. Perplexity — Best for web research

[Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) is an AI-powered answer and research product. It searches for information and presents conversational responses with links to sources.

**What you can use it for:**
- Explore current topics.
- Find and compare information from websites.
- Research products and services.
- Prepare an initial market or competitor overview.
- Discover sources for further reading.

**Practical example:** When researching a technology trend, Perplexity can help gather an initial overview and point you towards relevant sources.

**Important:** Citations are not a guarantee of accuracy. Open the original sources and verify important claims, dates and numbers.

**Free or paid?** A free plan is available with limits. Paid plans provide additional capabilities and higher usage.

**Best for:** Students, bloggers, marketers, researchers and professionals.

## 6. NotebookLM — Best for learning from your own documents

[NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/) is Google's research assistant for working with sources you provide. You can add supported documents and other sources, then ask questions grounded in that material.

**What you can use it for:**
- Summarise your study material.
- Ask questions about uploaded sources.
- Organise research notes.
- Create study guides and revision material.
- Generate Audio Overviews where supported.
- Understand lengthy documents.

**Practical example:** A college student can add unit-wise notes and ask NotebookLM to summarise a topic or create revision questions from those sources.

**Free or paid?** NotebookLM offers free access with usage limits. Additional limits and features may depend on available Google plans.

**Best for:** Students, teachers, researchers and professionals who work with source documents.

## 7. Elicit — Best for academic research

[Elicit](https://elicit.com/) is an AI research assistant focused on scientific literature. It can help researchers discover papers and organise information from academic studies.

**What you can use it for:**
- Search academic literature.
- Summarise research papers.
- Extract information from studies.
- Compare findings across papers.
- Support literature reviews.

**Practical example:** For a college project on machine learning, Elicit can help identify relevant papers and organise their findings. Read the original papers before relying on a summary.

**Free or paid?** Elicit offers a free plan with limits and paid plans for additional research capacity.

**Best for:** University students, academic researchers and people preparing literature reviews.

## 8. Canva AI — Best for graphic design

[Canva](https://www.canva.com/) is an online design platform with AI-assisted features. It helps people create visual content without needing advanced design software skills.

**What you can use it for:**
- Create social media graphics.
- Design presentations.
- Prepare posters and banners.
- Generate or edit visual assets with supported AI tools.
- Create marketing materials.

**Practical example:** A small business can create social media posts, promotional banners and presentation slides using templates and Canva's design tools.

**Free or paid?** Canva has a free plan. Some AI features, premium assets and higher usage limits require an eligible paid plan.

**Best for:** Small businesses, students, marketers and content creators.

## 9. Midjourney — Best for AI image generation

[Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/) generates images from text prompts. It is often used to explore artistic styles, campaign concepts and visual directions.

**What you can use it for:**
- Generate images from descriptions.
- Explore different visual styles.
- Create concept art and illustrations.
- Produce visual ideas for campaigns.
- Iterate on creative directions.

**Practical example:** A marketing team can describe a campaign concept and generate visual directions before creating the final approved design.

**Free or paid?** Midjourney generally requires a paid subscription. Check its official website for current plans and usage rules.

**Best for:** Designers, artists, creative agencies and marketing teams.

## 10. Adobe Firefly — Best for creative workflows

[Adobe Firefly](https://firefly.adobe.com/) is Adobe's family of generative AI tools for creating and editing visual content.

**What you can use it for:**
- Generate images from text prompts.
- Modify visual elements.
- Expand image compositions.
- Create creative assets.
- Use generative features in supported Adobe workflows.

**Practical example:** A designer can generate a background concept, adjust the composition and use the result as part of a larger design workflow.

**Free or paid?** Access and generative credits depend on the available free allowance and Adobe plan.

**Best for:** Designers, photographers, content creators and marketing teams.

## 11. Runway — Best for AI video generation

[Runway](https://runwayml.com/) provides AI-powered video generation and creative tools. Depending on the model and plan, users can generate video from text or images and experiment with visual scenes.

**What you can use it for:**
- Generate video clips from prompts.
- Animate still images.
- Explore visual scenes and camera movements.
- Create creative concepts.
- Support parts of a video production workflow.

**Practical example:** A creator can use an image and a carefully written prompt to generate a short visual clip for a concept video.

**Free or paid?** Access is plan- and credit-dependent. Check current model availability, credits and commercial-use terms before starting a project.

**Best for:** Video creators, filmmakers, advertisers and creative agencies.

## 12. ElevenLabs — Best for AI voice generation

[ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) provides AI voice and audio tools. It can turn written scripts into spoken audio and support other voice-related workflows.

**What you can use it for:**
- Convert text into speech.
- Create voiceovers.
- Produce supported multilingual audio.
- Support dubbing workflows.
- Create audio for videos and learning material.

**Practical example:** A video creator can prepare a script and generate a voiceover rather than recording every line manually.

**Free or paid?** A free tier is available with limits. Paid plans provide additional usage and features.

**Best for:** YouTubers, podcasters, educators and content creators.

**Responsible use:** Only clone or imitate a real person's voice when you have the appropriate permission.

## 13. Synthesia — Best for AI avatar videos

[Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/) helps users create videos with AI presenters and avatars from written scripts.

**What you can use it for:**
- Create presenter-style videos.
- Turn scripts into video presentations.
- Prepare training material.
- Produce educational content.
- Create supported multilingual videos.

**Practical example:** A company can prepare an employee onboarding video by writing a script and selecting an available AI presenter.

**Free or paid?** Synthesia offers a free option with limits. Paid plans provide additional video capacity and features.

**Best for:** Businesses, educators, HR teams and training departments.

## 14. n8n — Best for workflow automation

[n8n](https://n8n.io/) is a workflow automation platform that connects applications and services. Its visual workflow builder and integrations can also be used to build AI-powered processes.

**What you can use it for:**
- Connect different applications.
- Automate repetitive business processes.
- Build workflows using visual nodes.
- Integrate AI models and APIs.
- Process incoming data.
- Send automated notifications.

**Practical example:** When a website receives a new enquiry, an n8n workflow can process the form, save the information to a connected system and notify the team.

**Free or paid?** n8n offers cloud plans and a self-hosted Community Edition. Self-hosting can avoid a cloud subscription for the software edition, but hosting, maintenance and some integrations may still cost money.

**Best for:** Developers, startups, automation specialists and businesses.

## 15. Zapier — Best for no-code automation

[Zapier](https://zapier.com/) connects supported applications and automates tasks through workflows called Zaps. It is designed for people who want to connect tools without building a custom integration from scratch.

**What you can use it for:**
- Connect supported applications.
- Transfer information between services.
- Trigger actions when an event occurs.
- Automate repetitive tasks.
- Build workflows with supported AI steps.

**Practical example:** When a customer submits a contact form, Zapier can add the details to a spreadsheet and send a notification to the business owner.

**Free or paid?** Zapier has a limited free plan. Paid plans provide more tasks and advanced workflow capabilities.

**Best for:** Freelancers, business owners, marketers and non-technical users.

## Are free AI tools enough?

For many beginners, students and small businesses, free plans are enough to explore AI and complete basic tasks.

A practical starter setup could be:

- **ChatGPT or Gemini:** General assistance and learning.
- **Perplexity:** Initial web research.
- **NotebookLM:** Studying your own documents.
- **Canva:** Social media graphics and presentations.
- **n8n:** Experimenting with workflow automation.

Remember that free plans may have daily or monthly limits. Some advanced models, higher generation limits, team features and professional usage rights may require a paid subscription.

Before paying, test the free option and check whether the tool solves a recurring problem.

## How businesses can use AI tools

Businesses should not adopt AI simply because a tool is trending. First identify the task that takes too much time or creates unnecessary manual work.

For example:

1. **Content creation:** Use an AI assistant to prepare a first draft, then review and edit it. Use a design tool for the visual assets.
2. **Customer enquiries:** Explore a chatbot or an automated workflow for collecting and routing enquiries.
3. **Marketing research:** Use research tools to discover trends and sources, then verify the information.
4. **Internal processes:** Use n8n or Zapier to connect apps and reduce repetitive data entry.
5. **Training and communication:** Use voice or avatar-video tools where they genuinely improve the learning experience.

Keep a human review step for customer-facing, financial, legal and other important decisions. Also check data privacy policies before uploading confidential business or customer information to an AI service.

## Final thoughts

AI tools can help students learn, professionals work more efficiently and businesses improve selected processes. But choosing tools based on popularity alone is not a good strategy.

Start with the task, compare the available options and test whether the tool produces reliable results. Review important outputs, protect sensitive information and understand the limits of each free plan.

**Our recommendation:** Begin with one general-purpose AI assistant and one specialised tool for your main task. Expand your toolkit only when you have a clear reason.

## Frequently asked questions

### 1. What is the best AI tool in 2027?

There is no single best tool for every task. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are general-purpose assistants, while Perplexity, Midjourney and Runway focus on more specialised use cases.

### 2. Which AI tools have free plans?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, NotebookLM, Elicit and Canva offer free access or free plans with limits. n8n also offers a self-hosted Community Edition. Availability and limits can change.

### 3. Which AI tool is useful for students?

ChatGPT and Gemini can help explain concepts, NotebookLM can help study your own notes, and Elicit can support academic literature research. Students should follow their institution's rules for AI use and cite original sources.

### 4. Can AI tools help small businesses?

Yes. They can assist with content drafting, research, design, customer communication and workflow automation. Businesses should review outputs and protect confidential information.

### 5. Are AI-generated answers always accurate?

No. AI tools can make mistakes, omit context or present incorrect information confidently. Verify important facts against reliable original sources.

### 6. Do I need paid AI subscriptions?

Not necessarily. Start with free plans and upgrade only when you regularly reach usage limits or need a specific paid feature.
