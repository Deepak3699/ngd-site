# How Much Does an AI Chatbot / Automation Cost for a Small Business in India? (2026)

"AI chatbot" can mean a ₹10,000 FAQ bot or a ₹10 lakh commerce agent. Both are called chatbots, but they have almost nothing in common. If you are a small business in India planning automation in 2026, the useful question is not "what does a chatbot cost?" — it is **"what does the tier I actually need cost, per month, all-in?"**

This guide breaks down real 2026 costs: setup fees, monthly running costs, Meta's per-message charges, and worked budgets you can plan with.

## Cost tiers: setup + monthly running cost

| Chatbot type | Setup cost | Monthly running cost | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rule-based FAQ / lead bot | ₹10,000–₹15,000 | ₹2,000–₹5,000 | Simple FAQs, lead capture |
| Website LLM widget | ₹20,000–₹30,000 | ₹3,000–₹8,000 | Website support, general questions |
| WhatsApp RAG bot (answers from your data) | ₹35,000–₹1,00,000 | ₹5,000–₹15,000 | Accurate answers from your catalogue/documents |
| Full system (web + WhatsApp + CRM) | ₹85,000+ | ₹10,000–₹25,000 | End-to-end enquiry handling |
| Custom commerce bot (payments + CRM) | ₹1.5–15 lakh | Tokens + hosting extra | High-volume sales automation |

These are illustrative planning ranges, not verified market rates or a quote. Subscription products may cost less to start, while custom integrations can cost substantially more. Ask providers what setup, support and usage the price includes.

## The per-message layer: WhatsApp costs

WhatsApp Business Platform charges depend on message category, destination, applicable service windows and current pricing rules. Your provider may also add subscription fees or markups. Rates and tax treatment can change, so check [Meta's current pricing](https://business.whatsapp.com/products/platform-pricing) and get an itemised provider quote before budgeting.

## Worked monthly budget

Suppose a provider quotes ₹3,000 per month for its platform, ₹1,000 for message usage and ₹500 for AI usage. The estimated total is **₹4,500 per month before any applicable taxes and other charges**, plus any setup fee. This is an example, not a market quote: actual costs depend on volume and contract terms. Confirm whether platform fees already include message charges to avoid double-counting.

## Hidden costs people miss

- **LLM tokens:** AI replies cost per token. At small volumes this is minor; at scale, budget ₹5,000–₹40,000 per month.
- **Hosting and vector database:** ₹4,000–₹25,000 per month for custom RAG systems.
- **Template approval and maintenance:** WhatsApp templates need approval and occasional updates.
- **Content maintenance:** your catalogue, prices and FAQs change — someone must keep the bot's knowledge current.
- **Human handover:** a bot that cannot reach a human quickly is a liability, not an asset.

## SaaS vs custom: how to choose

- **Choose SaaS** if you have under 5,000 conversations a month and standard flows (auto-replies, follow-ups, broadcasts). You will be live in days for ₹1,000–₹12,000 per month.
- **Choose custom** if you need deep integrations (CRM, inventory, payments), strict data privacy, or volume that makes per-message markups painful. Expect ₹45,000+ setup for a production WhatsApp RAG bot.

## The ROI check

A well-designed chatbot can answer some enquiries outside business hours and help schedule follow-ups, but its accuracy and reliability must be monitored. If your average customer is worth ₹5,000 and the bot converts just 10 extra enquiries a month, that is **₹50,000 of additional revenue against a ₹10,000 cost** before fulfilment costs and other expenses. Revenue is not profit, and extra conversions are not guaranteed. That is the math to test against your own numbers before you buy anything.

## Final thoughts

In India in 2026, a small business can start WhatsApp automation for a few thousand rupees a month, and a serious AI chatbot system typically runs ₹5,000–₹25,000 per month plus setup. The right tier depends on your conversation volume, how accurate answers must be, and how deeply you integrate with your existing process. Start small, measure conversions, and upgrade when the numbers justify it.
